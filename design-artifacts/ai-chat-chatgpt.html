🧮 Consulta generada

Armé esta consulta para obtenerlo — corre solo lectura sobre shop_prod:

sql · generated 
1SELECT p.name, sum(oi.qty) AS units, sum(oi.qty * oi.unit_price) AS revenue
2FROM public.orders o
3JOIN public.order_items oi ON oi.order_id = o.id
4JOIN public.products p ON p.id = oi.product_id
5WHERE o.status = 'paid'
6  AND o.created_at >= now() - interval '7 days'
7GROUP BY p.name
8ORDER BY revenue DESC;
